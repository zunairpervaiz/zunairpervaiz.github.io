About me

With 13 years of total software engineering experience, I combine 9 years specializing in scalable mobile architecture (Flutter, Kotlin, Java, Swift) with a solid foundation of 4 years in full-stack backend and web systems (.NET, Laravel, JavaScript). My background bridges platform-level internals—including open-source contributions to the Android OS / AOSP ecosystem—with enterprise-grade mobile systems.

Currently working as a Team Lead and Project Manager, I steer cross-functional engineering teams, enforce clean architecture, eliminate performance bottlenecks, and align complex technical execution with business strategy for reliable, on-time delivery.

I graduated as a Silver Medalist (3.93 CGPA) in Information Technology from GCU Faisalabad and previously served as a Google DSC Lead. As a technical educator, I also run @baabadevs on YouTube, sharing in-depth Flutter tutorials, architecture patterns, and engineering insights with the developer community.
